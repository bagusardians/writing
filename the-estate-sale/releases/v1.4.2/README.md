## What changed from v1.4.1

One continuity fix, from a first-reader note on Frame One.

In v1.4.1 the narrator says she attended the Kanzaki sale as a *buyer* working for no one, yet a
later paragraph had her learn from the sale listing that the mirror's buyer paid cash, in person,
an hour after she left, and asked for the tag to be left on. A public listing shows only a lot
number, a buyer's initial, and a deposit date; it would never show those details. The insider
facts now come from the right place: she calls the sale, and the son who ran it tells her, buyer
to seller. This keeps her role consistent with the rest of the chapter.
