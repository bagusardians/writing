Word counts are computed on the same basis as v1.1.0 only where noted; v1.1.0's 50,326
included the assembled total on the original assembly method. From v1.2.0 onward the figure
is words excluding image prompts, the same basis used to measure v1.1.0 at 44,951. v1.2.0
(46,666) is the continuity revision. v1.3.0 (44,955) is the page-turner pass. v1.4.0 (44,955)
is the illustration-specification pass: prose unchanged, plate labels made unique across the
book, and every prompt made standalone and structured. v1.4.1 (45,530) is the post-review
continuity-and-ending revision: the ending was rebuilt so the narrator refuses the gap and
survives (rather than both dying and narrating), the blank-tag reveal now resolves to the role
rather than to a specific unborn person, the two hands no longer overlap, the lineage and year
threads are reconciled, and two pre-existing plate-label/content rotations (chapter 15 in the
sheet, chapter 20 in the manuscript) are corrected so captions and prompts are one-to-one.

Working drafts and the editable chapter files live in `../phase-6-draft/`. A release is a
frozen copy assembled from those chapters; it is not edited in place. To revise, produce a
new version folder rather than editing a release.
