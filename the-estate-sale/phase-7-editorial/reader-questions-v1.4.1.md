# Reader coherence & understandability questions — full sequential read (v1.4.1)

Read chapter by chapter. Questions are from a first-reader's perspective: "does this make
sense", "who is this person", "why is this not explained". Tagged [BLOCKER] (stops
comprehension), [CONFUSION] (pulls the reader out), [NIT].

## Frame One — The Tag
- Q1 [CONFUSION] "Her son had emptied the house" — who is her son? (Kei, not named until later.)
- Q4 [BLOCKER] "two hundred lots" vs "two hundred and eleven tags" in the same chapter.
- Q5 [CONFUSION] The finder's note says the writer wrote in "a notebook, a folder of statements,
  and a phone". Which chapters are which? Not told.

## 1. The Mirror
- Q6 [BLOCKER] Who is this narrator? The killer says her mother "took it from the house in 1958.
  The servant's daughter", which implies Hana's granddaughter = Aoi. But Aoi survives; this
  narrator dies. Cannot both be true.
- Q7 [BLOCKER] "there are only two pieces left, and you're one of them" — who is the other?
  Never explained; by the end only Aoi is "the last piece".
- Q8 [CONFUSION] "The photographer asks me to tilt my chin" — who is the photographer? The
  narrator is holding the phone.
- Q9 [NIT] Narrator has "worked with antiques for eleven years"; Aoi also says eleven years.

## 2. The Coat
- Q10 [BLOCKER] "the tag did not name his grandfather... It named whoever had first borne the
  name" contradicts the earlier "the tag on the stand was his grandfather's name".
- Q11 [CONFUSION] Yano reads the coat's tag (Kuroda) then the stand's tag (Yano). Two tags;
  easy to conflate.
- Q12 [CONFUSION] Kuroda Genji is met first as a drowning victim, later as the attacker.
- Q13 [NIT] "the photograph... was a photograph of him dead" — did he die, or sit down?

## 3. The Music Box
- Q14 [BLOCKER] Sakai is one of the four dead buyers, but the chapter ends with him alive at
  Aoi's flat. How/when he dies is never shown.
- Q15 [CONFUSION] "the box only ever plays eight [bars]" but "the tune has twelve bars".
- Q16 [NIT] Kei: "There were four of them. You have the last one." Four boxes? Where are the rest?

## 4. Frame Two — The Estate
- Q17 [CONFUSION] "She started in 1961... She was fourteen" vs Chapter 17's "thirteen in 1961".
- Q18 [CONFUSION] Mitsuko died "at seventy-eight" but born 1947 makes her 76.
- Q19 [BLOCKER] Frame Two ends with the room drawn in Aoi's dust; Chapter 5 (The Keys) is the
  locksmith's story with no time anchor.
- Q20 [CONFUSION] "The dream came in June... Mikami had it in October." Frame Two is May–June.

## 5. The Keys
- Q21 [BLOCKER] The client "has not told me his name." Kuroda Jun, the came-after, or other?
  Never resolved. Described exactly like the grey-coat man.
- Q22 [CONFUSION] "the filed were keys that fit a false tumbril" — should be "tumbler".

## 6. The Ledger
- Q25 [CONFUSION] "It's the date the seller died" vs later "when the holder arrived... when they
  died, or when they were filed". Two definitions.
- Q26 [CONFUSION] "the year at the very top of the second column was not a date at all" — what
  was it? Not shown.

## 7. Interlude
- Q28 [CONFUSION] "I noticed the pattern in May. Four deaths, one a month." The four deaths run
  April–June; in May there were not yet four.

## 8. The Album
- Q30 [CONFUSION] "born around 1951 — which made her eleven, if the room dates from 1958."
  Born 1951 = 7 in 1958. Should be born ~1947.
- Q31 [CONFUSION] How the envelope was posted is split across Ch8 and Ch15.
- Q32 [NIT] "whose grandmother the nurse next door had been" — the nurse has not appeared yet.

## 9. Frame Three — The Method
- Q33 [BLOCKER] "Eleven stories. Eleven people." Which eleven, exactly? Not listed.
- Q34 [CONFUSION] "Four are dead: Yano, Sakai, Ito, Suzuki" — the reader never saw Sakai or Ito
  die.
- Q35 [CONFUSION] "Two are filed: Mikami, Kubo" — Kubo's story (Ch12) comes after this frame.
- Q36 [CONFUSION] "the screen restorer's screen" — Yamamura (Ch13) also comes later.

## 10. The Tea Set
- Q38 [BLOCKER] Reiko "I am forty-one" but "I wrote back. I was twenty-six" in 1989 → 60.
- Q39 [CONFUSION] Kenji: "I have said it for thirty years" when Reiko is 14 (~1977); 1958+30=1988.

## 11. The Comb

- Q44 [CONFUSION] Hana dies when Aoi is 14 (~2008), but the came-after (Ch14) says he found the

## 18. The Visitor

## 19. Frame Four — The Measure
- Q65 [BLOCKER] "Mikami called on the second of November and said we had to go to Kuramae", then

  And Chapter 20 (December) intervenes before Chapter 21's trip.
- Q66 [CONFUSION] Who arranged the lots on the floor? Not said here.

## 20. The Tag Box
- Q68 [BLOCKER] The nineteen tags include SHIRAI AOI and SAKAGUCHI RIE. Sakaguchi died in
  November; Aoi is alive. If the tags are a death schedule, why does Aoi's remain?
- Q69 [CONFUSION] Route here is June–December; Chapter 4's deaths are April–June.
- Q70 [CONFUSION] "six of the nineteen dates had been corrected" — by whom, revealed only now?

## 21. The Room
- Q72 [CONFUSION] "Mitsuko did not kill me. Mitsuko finished me." Then "I was not the last victim

- Q73 [CONFUSION] "the blank tag... it was in my hand. I had picked it up and never put it down."

- Q75 [NIT] "I am the last piece" appears twice, near-verbatim.
- Q76 [NIT] The frame-within-frame ("recovered separately") is not clearly signposted.
