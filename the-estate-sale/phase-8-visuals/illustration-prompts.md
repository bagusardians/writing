# The Estate Sale — Illustration Prompt Sheet (Phase 8)

Prompts for image generation. The images are the primary clue medium: each must be
decodable from the prose. House style across all plates:

- **Overall look:** Japanese documentary / found-evidence aesthetic. Muted, desaturated,
  slightly muddy palette — greens, browns, grey-blues. Natural light or a single bulb.
  Slight grain, as if scanned or phone-shot. No saturated colours, no horror splatter.
- **Consistency anchors:** the tags are small, white, handwritten in a small upright hand.
  The tansu is dark wood with iron fittings. The six-mat room has no window. The mirror is
  black lacquer with a hairline crack down the glass.
- **Negative prompt (all):** gore, blood, visible wounds, gothic horror, red lighting,
  glowing eyes, ghosts, dolls with human faces, text errors, extra fingers, watermarks,
  modern logos, real brand marks, identifiable real people.

Each plate is labelled as it appears in the manuscript.

## Frame One — The Tag

**1.1 — The estate hall (photograph).** Wide documentary photograph of a cleared
warehouse used as an estate sale: eight trestle tables under bare hanging bulbs, neat rows
of tagged household objects (ceramics, lacquerware, small furniture), stacks of flattened
cardboard, a blurred crowd of anonymous buyers in coats. In sharp focus in the foreground,
one small white tag tied to a black-lacquer dressing mirror, handwriting legible. Cool,
dusty light; concrete floor. 3:2.

## 1. The Mirror

**2.1 — The fourth photograph (photograph).** A smartphone photo showing a woman's
reflection in an antique black-lacquer dressing mirror with a hairline crack. She holds a
phone at chest height. Behind her reflection, where the real wall is blank plaster, a low
pale doorway opens into a windowless six-mat room with a dark chest against the wall.
The doorway is slightly smaller than it should be, as if receding. Flat phone-flash
lighting, slight lens distortion. 3:4 vertical.

---

## 2. The Coat

**3.1 — The rack and the coat (photograph).** Interior of a small Japanese secondhand
shop: four clothing racks, a counter. Centred, a heavy grey Shōwa-era wool overcoat on a
wooden hanger, shoulders too wide. A small white tag tied to the hanger with cotton thread;
handwriting legible (KURODA / 1958 / YANAKA). Warm dim shop lighting. 3:2.

**3.2 — The daishō stand (photograph).** Back room, dim: a dark wooden daishō stand (two
horizontal sword hooks) on a shelf, dust. A small white tag tied on, reading YANO / 1958 /
YANAKA. Shot at an angle, low light, slight blur at the edges. 4:3.

**3.3 — The reverse of the tag (photograph).** Extreme close-up: a small white tag held in
an adult hand, blank side up but for faint pencil writing: "still here?" and a small date,
1958. Shallow depth of field; the fingers slightly out of focus. 3:2.

**3.4 — The shop at night (photograph).** Exterior of the shop across a narrow street at
night. A CLOSED sign hangs on the glass. Through the glass, the silhouette of one figure
standing where the counter is, facing the racks. In the window's reflection, one more
person is visible than the room contains. Cold streetlight, wet asphalt. 3:2.

---

## 3. The Music Box

**4.1 — The cylinder box (photograph).** A wooden cylinder music box on a table, lid open,
brass cylinder and comb visible, a small tag tied to the winding key. Focus on the comb
and pins; the room behind falls into darkness. Warm lamp light. 4:3.

**4.2 — The interior lid (photograph).** The same box photographed from directly above,
interior lid open: inside, a faint hand-drawn pencil diagram — a small six-mat room drawn
off-square, with a rectangle labelled with a single character against one wall (a tansu).
The drawing is faint, childlike, not architectural. 3:2.

**4.3 — The hallway at night (photograph).** A narrow Japanese hall at night: the music
box on a shelf, its tag legible. At the far end, a child's shadow falls on the wall — but
the photographer's own shadow is absent from the frame. Long exposure, deep darkness.
3:4 vertical.

**4.4 — The Yanesen map (hand-drawn).** A hand-drawn pencil map on aged paper showing the
Yanesen lanes: Yanaka Ginza, Nezu Shrine, temple walls, alleys. One lane behind the main
street is marked with a small tansu symbol and the date 1958. The hand is careful, slightly
uncertain. Flat scan. 4:3.

## Frame Two — The Estate

**5.1 — The warehouse (photograph).** Interior of a Kuramae warehouse: metal shelving
holding rows of tagged lots, a single hanging bulb, tags catching the light. In the
foreground, a cassette recorder on a wooden crate, its mechanical counter at 000. Dust,
cold air, wide angle. 3:2.

## 4. The Keys

**6.1 — The cold wall (photograph).** A narrow timber passage in an old Yanaka row-house:
plain plaster wall, a hand pressed flat against it. Beside the wall, pinned to the timber,
a hand-drawn floor plan with a storage room marked and the wall crossed out in ink.
Daylight from one side. 3:2.

Additional (locked-room interior) — **6.2 — Inside the room (photograph).** Inside a
six-mat windowless room whose walls are lined floor-to-ceiling with tagged objects (mirror,
coat on a hook, boxes, ceramics), arranged tightly, with an empty standing-place in the
centre of the floor. One open doorway behind, light spilling in. 3:4 vertical.

## 5. The Ledger

**7.1 — The open ledger (photograph).** A bound Japanese account book from the 1960s open
on a workbench to two facing pages: columns of purchases in a small upright hand, a right-
hand unlabelled column of dates that do not match the purchases; earliest entry 1961. White
cotton gloves at the edge of frame. Warm workshop light. 3:2.

**7.2 — The magnified line (photograph).** Macro detail of one ledger line: a purchase of a
lacquer comb in Sendagi, 1991; the unlabelled column reads 1991; a faint pencil tally in
the margin. Extreme shallow depth of field. 3:2.

**7.3 — The repair tickets (photograph).** A workshop wall with dozens of paper repair
tickets hung from strings, each dated. One ticket from 1991 centred and legible; beside it,
a small photograph of a woman's hands accepting a parcel, her face out of frame. 3:2.

## 6. The Album

**8.1 — The open album (photograph).** An open photograph album on a low table: black-and-
white prints corner-mounted on card, board cover water-stained, a blank slip of paper
between two pages. Soft window light. 3:2.

**8.2 — One album page (photograph).** Three black-and-white photographs of the same
six-mat windowless room from slightly different angles, mounted on one album page. The
objects on the walls differ between the three; the room does not. 4:3.

**8.3 — The annotated contact sheet (diagram/photograph).** A print of the album room
reproduced as a contact sheet, with ruled overlay lines and a handwritten calculation in
the margin dating the camera height to a child's eye level (c. 45 cm); the tansu is circled
in red pencil. 3:2.

**8.4 — The folded 1958 print (photograph).** A creased, tape-repaired black-and-white
1958 photograph: a bare six-mat room, objects gone; a man on the floor (not gory — a still
figure, face turned away); in the doorway, a blurred adult woman, and behind her, partially
hidden, the small outline of a child. 3:4 vertical.

## 7. The Tea Set

**9.1 — The cups (photograph).** A kitchen counter, close: a five-piece Japanese tea set
arranged in a row, each cup with a small handwritten tag tied to its handle, tags turned
to face away from the camera. Steam just beginning to rise. Warm domestic light. 3:2.

**9.2 — The powder (photograph, overhead).** A hand tipping a measured spoonful of white
powder into one cup, shot from directly above; the powder dissolves; the other four cups
already filled. A tag hangs visible on the poisoned cup's handle (TOYAMA / 1958 / YANAKA).
3:2.

**9.3 — The low table (photograph).** A low table seen from a doorway: five cups, two in
front of a seated elderly man, three untouched; the man upright, both hands flat on the
table. The narrator's hands hold a teapot at the frame's edge, steady. Warm, ordinary,
domestic. 3:2.

**9.4 — After (photograph).** The tea set after: four cups emptied, one full; the elderly
man slumped forward face down (restrained, not gory); a hand enters from the frame's edge
to turn his cup's tag face-up, showing the name. 3:2.

## 8. The Comb

**10.1 — The ticket drawer (photograph).** A workshop drawer open to the light: hundreds
of paper repair tickets on loops of string. The camera isolates one, dated 1991, for a
lacquer comb, with the owner's surname legible as HANA in a small upright hand. 3:2.

**10.2 — The comb and the receipt (photograph).** A lacquer comb on a bench, restored, one
tooth replaced in slightly different lacquer; beside it a receipt book open to a page where
the name HANA appears in pencil. 3:2.

**10.3 — The route map (hand-drawn).** Pencil map of the Sendagi and Yanaka streets with a
route drawn from a timber row-house to a small apartment block three streets away; dated
1991, with the names MIKAMI and HANA at either end. Flat scan. 4:3.

## 9. The Doll

**11.1 — The doll (photograph).** A bisque-headed doll in traditional Japanese costume, on
a workbench, one hand repaired; a small tag tied at the wrist in the small upright hand
(HANA / 1958 / YANAKA). The doll's face is turned away from the camera. 3:2.

**11.2 — The paper strip (photograph).** A folded paper strip unfolded: a child's
handwriting, a numbered list of twelve names, the last set below a ruled line in a steadier
hand, reading SHIRAI. Paper water-stained, sewn-edge frayed. Macro. 3:2.

**11.3 — The shelf at dawn (photograph).** A shelf in a repair shop at dawn: an open box, a
doll inside now facing outward toward the door; its wrist tag turned blank-side-up; a strip
of paper on the shelf below. Cold blue dawn light. 3:2.

**11.4 — The Tateishi alley (photograph).** A narrow yokochō drinking alley at night,
lantern-lit, wet. In the far frame, a child-sized figure seen only from behind, walking
away. A shadow enters the bottom of the frame, implying the photographer. 3:4 vertical.

## 10. The Tag Box

**12.1 — The flea-market table (photograph).** A December flea market (Bōro-ichi): a
folding table crowded with kitchen knives, dead clocks, ceramics; a lacquered box open
among them holding a stack of small blank inventory tags, string still coiled; one tag
lifted slightly showing pencil on its reverse. Overcast daylight, crowds blurred behind.
3:2.

**12.2 — The pairing chart (diagram).** A hand-drawn two-column chart on paper. Left:
the twelve 1958 names with their front dates. Right: nineteen living names with faint
future dates. Hand-drawn arrows connect a 1958 name to a living name, forming a chain.
Pencil, ruled by hand. Flat scan. 3:2.

## The Room (Unlock)

**13.1 — The warehouse room (photograph).** A Kuramae warehouse interior, wide: two
hundred tagged objects arranged on the floor to form the outline of a six-mat room; a low
doorway marked by two upright posts. From the camera's height, the arrangement reads as a
floor plan. Two small figures stand at the edge. One hanging bulb. 3:2.

**13.2 — The measured plan (diagram).** A measured floor plan, hand-annotated: an eight-by-
fourteen storage room overlaid with a seven-by-twelve arrangement; the discrepancy shaded;
a note reading "eye height ≈ 45 cm"; a small tansu symbol in the corner. Flat scan. 4:3.

**13.3 — The threshold (photograph).** The warehouse from the doorway: the arranged room;
a well-dressed man standing at the threshold; two small figures inside the arrangement; a
single hanging bulb; the man's long shadow falling across the tags on the floor toward the
standing-place. 3:2.

**13.4 — The phone screen (photograph).** A smartphone screen photographed: a camera roll,
one image selected. In that image, a woman's reflection in a cracked mirror; behind her a
low pale doorway, and beyond it a windowless six-mat room whose wall holds a row of tags.
The most recent tag has no name — only a date. It is today. 3:4 vertical, slight screen
glare.
