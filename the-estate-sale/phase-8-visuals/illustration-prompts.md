# The Estate Sale — Illustration Prompt Sheet (Phase 8)

Prompts for image generation. The images are the primary clue medium: each must be
decodable from the prose. Every plate corresponds to an inline caption in the manuscript,
and every chapter carries five or more plates, so a reader can solve the crime from the
images alone.

## House style

- **Overall look:** Japanese documentary / found-evidence aesthetic. Muted, desaturated,
  slightly muddy palette — greens, browns, grey-blues. Natural light or a single bulb.
  Slight grain, as if scanned or phone-shot. No saturated colours, no horror splatter.
- **Consistency anchors:** the tags are small, white, handwritten in a small upright hand.
  The tansu is dark wood with iron fittings. The six-mat room has no window. The mirror is
  black lacquer with a hairline crack down the glass.
- **Medium mix:** photographs are the primary medium; floor plans, maps, and hand-drawn
  diagrams carry the measured clues; hand-drawn sketches appear among the documents.
- **Negative prompt (all plates):** gore, blood, visible wounds, gothic horror, red
  lighting, glowing eyes, ghosts, dolls with human faces, text errors, extra fingers,
  watermarks, modern logos, real brand marks, identifiable real people.

## How to read a plate

Each entry gives the plate label, its medium and aspect ratio, the subject, and the camera
and lighting. Apply the house style and the negative prompt to every plate. Labels are
`<chapter>.<plate>`; the chapter number matches the filename prefix.
