# The Estate Sale — Illustration Prompt Sheet (Phase 8)

Prompts for image generation. The images are the primary clue medium: each plate must be
decodable from the prose. Every prompt below is **standalone** — it restates the full house
style, the world anchors, the negative prompt, and the chapter's continuity anchors, so any
single prompt can be pasted into an image model on its own and still yield an on-style,
on-model result.

## Numbering

Plate labels are `<chapter>.<plate>` and are unique across the whole book: the chapter number
matches the filename prefix, and the plate number runs from 1 in reading order within the
chapter. The same label is used in the manuscript caption and in this sheet.

## House style (embedded in every full prompt)

Japanese documentary / found-evidence aesthetic. Muted, desaturated, low-contrast palette of greens, browns and grey-blues, slightly muddy, with the gentle grain and softness of a scanned print or a phone snapshot. Natural daylight or a single practical bulb; no coloured gels, no horror lighting, no stylisation. Restrained, clinical framing that treats every object as a piece of evidence rather than a spectacle. Shallow or moderate depth of field, natural perspective, no wide-angle distortion unless noted.

**World anchors.** Recurring world anchors (keep identical across all plates): the tags are small, white, rectangular, and handwritten in the same small upright hand in dark pencil or ink; the tansu is dark wood with iron fittings; the six-mat room has no window and a pale doorway; the mirror is black lacquer with a hairline crack down the glass; the light is Tokyo winter.

**Negative prompt.** Avoid: gore, blood, visible wounds, gothic horror, red lighting, glowing eyes, ghosts, dolls with human faces, deformed hands, extra fingers, garbled or misspelled text, watermarks, modern logos, real brand marks, identifiable real people, saturated colour, high-contrast drama, lens flare.
